# Larkspur Logistics: warehouse relocation memo

Internal, September 2026.

The warehouse relocation project is code-named BLUE-OTTER-73. It moves Larkspur Logistics' cold store from Dock Road to the Hale Park estate.

## Timetable

- The new building is handed over on 12 January 2027.
- Racking and refrigeration are fitted over the following six weeks.
- Stock moves over two weekends in March 2027, and Dock Road closes on 31 March 2027.

## Budget

The relocation budget is £3.4 million, of which £1.1 million is refrigeration.
