## Design Context

The creator-approved context is recorded in DESIGN.md; this feature follows it.

### Users
Researchers and technical knowledge workers reading and writing with their own
documents. Library groups help them organise a mixed English/Chinese collection.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, trustworthy. Quiet paper and ink; the documents take precedence.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the existing light desktop interface, Source Sans 3 for controls and Source
Serif 4 for writing, neutral surfaces, 1px rules and restrained blue interaction
states. Follow DESIGN.md's existing tokens and components.

### Design Principles
- Flat, readable lists with clear names and descriptions.
- Keep classification setup optional and progressively disclosed.
- Show pending work and failures in words; preserve manual choices.
- Reuse the application's keyboard, focus and bilingual copy patterns.
