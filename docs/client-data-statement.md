For a practice that uses IncarnaMind on client work: what to tell clients about their documents, in paragraphs you can paste into an engagement letter. It describes how the app behaves. Check it against the version you install, and see [privacy.md](privacy.md) for the full detail.

## For the practice (not for the letter)

Paste the paragraphs under "For your engagement letter" and fill in the brackets.
- **Before you send it**, open Settings › Privacy in the app: it lists every service that is on.
- **Never promise "no training" on a provider's behalf.** Keep only the route line that applies. Some providers train on API data unless you opt out, so read your provider's terms. If you use "Use my ChatGPT plan", check OpenAI's terms for ChatGPT plan use.
- **Also good to know:**
  - Deleted items stay on your computer for 30 days.
  - Older versions of a quoted document are kept so Citations still open.
  - Meeting recordings and saved web pages are kept in the app's data folder on your computer.
  - If you turn on an optional search service (for example Cohere or Voyage), it receives search matches. If you turn on cloud search (hosted embeddings), the full text of your documents goes to that service.
  - A searchable text copy of each document is kept in the app's data folder.
  - After 30 days a deleted item leaves only a small record that it existed.
  - Usage data and crash reports are off unless you agree; test builds you were invited to may send usage data by default.
  - Connectors you add may still be reached when "Keep everything on this computer" is on.

## For your engagement letter

**How we use IncarnaMind.** We use IncarnaMind, an open-source desktop application, to check our written work against your documents. It runs on our own computers.

**Where your documents are kept.** Your documents stay on our computers, in the folders where we keep them. IncarnaMind has no account and no server for your documents. By design, its maker never receives your documents or our questions about them.

**What is sent, and to whom.** To answer a question or check our work, IncarnaMind sends only the parts the task needs to an AI model service we have chosen: [the provider, for example Anthropic]. That means the text we are asking about or checking, the matching passages of your documents with their names and pages, and the names of other documents it could search. To file your documents, it may also send a document's name, its outline and short excerpts, and for a scanned page with no text, up to three page images. If we turn on cloud search, the full text of your documents goes to that service. Nothing is sent to a service until we have chosen it in the application.

**Training and retention.** IncarnaMind never trains on your documents. What a model service keeps, and whether it trains on what it receives, depends on how we use it. [Keep the line that applies.]
- [With our own API key:] [the provider]'s API terms apply: [the provider's data terms, or "see [the provider]'s API terms"].
- [With a ChatGPT plan:] OpenAI's terms for use through a ChatGPT plan apply.
- [With "Keep everything on this computer" on and a model running on our own computers:] your documents and our questions are not sent to any AI model service.

**Your choice.** If you would rather your documents were not sent to any model service, tell us. We will check our work with a model that runs on our own computers, or without AI.
