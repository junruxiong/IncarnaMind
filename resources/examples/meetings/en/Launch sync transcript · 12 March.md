# Launch sync, Thursday 12 March

Recorded call of the Fernwood Pantry launch team. Fernwood Pantry is a made-up company, and everyone in this call is made up too. Attendees: Lena Brandt (product), Tomás Ferreira (engineering), Priya Nair (marketing), Ian Walsh (finance). Recording starts at 10:00.

## 10:00 Opening

[10:00:12] Lena: Morning, everyone. Three things today: where the app launch stands, the price of the starter box, and who owns what for the next two weeks.

[10:00:41] Tomás: Before we start, a quick note. The recording is on, so if anyone wants something kept out of the notes, say so now.

[10:00:55] Lena: Good. Nobody? Then let's go.

## 10:02 App launch status

[10:02:08] Tomás: The ordering flow passed testing on Tuesday. We found one bug, where the delivery date reset when someone changed their postcode. It is fixed, and the fix will ship in build 41.

[10:03:30] Lena: So are we still on track for the launch on 7 April?

[10:03:44] Tomás: Yes, as long as the payment provider approves our account by 27 March. They told us five working days, and we applied on 11 March, so the answer should come by 18 March. We have plenty of room.

[10:05:02] Lena: Good. Let's treat 27 March as the deadline anyway. If the approval isn't through by then, we tell the board that day, not later.

[10:06:15] Priya: Can I ask about the waiting list? Marketing needs to know when to start sending the early-access emails.

[10:06:40] Tomás: Build 41 goes to the waiting list first. I'd say 30 March for the first 500 people.

[10:07:10] Lena: Agreed. First 500 people on 30 March, everyone else on launch day.

## 10:09 Starter box price

[10:09:20] Ian: I ran the numbers again. At 34 euros the starter box makes about 6 euros per box after packaging and delivery. At 29 euros it loses 1 euro per box.

[10:10:05] Priya: The survey said people would pay 29. Only a third said they would pay 34.

[10:10:48] Ian: A third is still 330 of the 1,000 people we asked. And the first box is not where we make money. The second and third orders are.

[10:12:00] Lena: What if we keep 34 euros but make the first box 10 euros cheaper with a welcome code?

[10:12:35] Ian: That works. A first box at 24 euros loses 4 euros, but if half the customers order again, we get it back by the third box.

[10:13:30] Priya: And a welcome code is easy to explain in the ads. I like it.

[10:14:05] Lena: Decision, then: the starter box costs 34 euros, and new customers get 10 euros off their first box with a welcome code. Ian, please write down the numbers so we can check them in a month.

[10:14:30] Ian: Will do, by Monday.

## 10:16 Who owns what

[10:16:10] Lena: Next two weeks. Tomás, you ship build 41 and chase the payment approval.

[10:16:30] Tomás: Yes. I also need Priya's final delivery-area list by Wednesday, because the postcode check depends on it.

[10:16:55] Priya: You'll have it Tuesday. I will also send the early-access email text to Lena for review by 25 March.

[10:17:30] Lena: Fine. Ian, the price numbers by Monday. I will tell the board about the 7 April date and the price on Friday.

[10:18:20] Ian: One more thing. The supplier asked whether we can raise the first order of vegetables from 800 to 1,200 kilos.

[10:18:50] Lena: Not until the waiting list shows real demand. Tell them 800, and we revisit on 8 April, the day after launch.

## 10:20 Close

[10:20:05] Lena: That's everything. Next sync is Thursday 19 March, same time. Thanks, all.
